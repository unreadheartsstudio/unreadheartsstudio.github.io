This Privacy Policy explains how BL Romance Anime Uploader (the "App"), operated by the YouTube channel 心動BL劇場 BL Romance Anime (@blromanceanime.studio), handles data. The App is an internal tool used only by the channel owner.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses the YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What data the App accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the App accesses only the following, and only for the channel owner's own YouTube channel:

  • Basic channel information (channel ID, name, subscriber and video counts).
  • The channel's own videos: uploading new videos and setting their titles, descriptions, translations, tags, category, privacy status, audience and altered-content settings.
  • Caption tracks for the channel's own videos (uploading subtitle files).
  • The channel's own playlists (creating the series playlist and adding the channel's videos to it).
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel (views, watch time, audience retention and similar aggregate metrics).

The App does not access any other channel's data, does not read comments or private messages, and does not collect data from viewers.

3. How the data is used

The data is used solely to publish and manage the channel's own animated episodes and to produce internal performance reports that help improve future episodes. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

  • The Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user account.
  • Analytics results are kept only as local reports on the same computer.
  • No data is stored on any server operated by the App, and this website itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

5. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, share or disclose any user data obtained through the YouTube API Services to any third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. The channel owner can delete the locally stored tokens and reports at any time. Access can also be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page; once revoked, the App can no longer access the account, and stored data is deleted within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
